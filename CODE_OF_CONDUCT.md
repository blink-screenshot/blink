# Code of conduct

Blink follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful of differing opinions and experience levels, accept constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people who use Blink. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project is represented.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the contact details on the [project's GitHub profile](https://github.com/blink-screenshot). All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break them.
